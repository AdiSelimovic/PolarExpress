Your Journey Route

Experience the journey north through winter wonderland destinations

New York City

Departure Point - Grand Central Station

Board the Polar Express at the historic Grand Central Terminal. The conductor will punch your golden ticket as you embark on your winter adventure north through stunning landscapes.

Adirondack Mountains, New York

210 miles north

Wind through ancient forests blanketed in pristine snow. The train climbs through mountain passes where evergreens stand tall and wildlife thrives in the winter wilderness.

Quebec City, Canada

405 miles north

Cross into Canada as temperatures drop and snow deepens. Pass through historic Quebec with its European charm, all dressed in holiday lights and winter splendor.

Churchill, Manitoba

1,485 miles north - Arctic Gateway

Arrive at Churchill, the "Polar Bear Capital of the World" on the shores of Hudson Bay. Experience true Arctic winter, witness the northern lights, and enjoy a magical holiday celebration in one of Canada's most remote and spectacular winter destinations.
