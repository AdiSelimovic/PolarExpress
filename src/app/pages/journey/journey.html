The Northern Route

All trains converge for the final journey to the Canadian Arctic

Winnipeg, Manitoba

Convergence Point - Union Station

All Polar Express trains converge at Winnipeg's historic Union Station. Here, passengers transfer to the special Arctic Express for the final leg of the journey north through the Canadian wilderness.

Thompson, Manitoba

400 miles north of Winnipeg

Journey through the boreal forest as civilization fades away. Thompson marks the gateway to the true north, where endless forests of spruce and pine stretch to the horizon.

Churchill, Manitoba

1,485 miles north - Arctic Gateway

Arrive at Churchill, the "Polar Bear Capital of the World" on the shores of Hudson Bay. Experience true Arctic winter, witness the northern lights, and enjoy a magical holiday celebration in one of Canada's most remote and spectacular winter destinations.
